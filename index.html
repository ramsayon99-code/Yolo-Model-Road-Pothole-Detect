How it works

📍 When you start the live camera, the browser asks for Location permission and Camera permission. Allow both for GPS coordinates and road scanning.

Images and live camera frames are resized to 640×640 and processed locally by the trained ONNX model in model/best.onnx. WebGPU is used when the phone browser supports it, with WASM fallback. Detection is intentionally throttled to keep the phone responsive; the Firebase report is written immediately after a confirmed detection, while the ImageBB image upload runs separately.
